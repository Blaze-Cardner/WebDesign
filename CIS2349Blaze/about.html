About Me

My name is Blaze Cardner and I am a CIS-System Development Major with a minor in Criminal Justice from Howard Payne University. I am 22 years old and I am from Flower Mound, Texas, just Northwest of Dallas. I am the son of William Cardner and Stephanie Murphy, and have one sister, Maraea, who is 28 and lives in New York City. I played collegiate baseball at North Arkansas College in Harrison, AR for a year where I was an Academic All American as well as an NJCAA 2nd Team All-American. I transferred to Howard Payne when I was 19 to play baseball, and through a series of unfortunate events found my true calling. In my free time, I enjoy going to the gym, golfing on the weekends, and hanging out with my friends, family, and fiancée.
